Welcome to My Hospital

🎮 How to play

  1. Choose how many patients and the mode, then press Start.
  2. Patients walk around and fall asleep by themselves. The staff carries them to a free bed, or to the hallway if every room is full.
  3. Auto simulation on: patients also wake up by themselves. Just watch.
  4. Auto simulation off (manual): waking them up is your job. Click a patient in a bed and press Wake up; they must have spent at least 5 s in the room.
  5. Extra: click an awake patient to trigger an attack, or a staff member to send them to rest.
  6. Pause freezes everything · Restart goes back to the welcome screen.
